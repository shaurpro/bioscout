# Technical discovery session — template

Use this for a real 30-minute conversation with a "customer" (for example a professor or senior from the
Biological Data Analytics minor, or a lab group). This is the part of a Forward Deployed Engineer's job
that happens before any code: understand the problem, the data and what "good" means.

## 1. Problem
- Who will use this? What question do they ask most often today, and how do they answer it now?
- How long does that take? What goes wrong?

## 2. Data
- Structured data (tables, CSVs, databases): what, how big, how often updated?
- Unstructured data (papers, PDFs, notes): what, how many, any access/licensing limits?

## 3. Success criteria
- What would make this useful on day one? (e.g. "answers with citations I can check")
- What is unacceptable? (e.g. made-up numbers, slow answers > 10 s)
- Budget: acceptable cost per question?

## 4. Constraints
- Where must it run (laptop, Google Cloud project)? Any data that cannot leave the institute?

## 5. Agreed next steps
- Questions for an evaluation set (write 10 real ones from the user)
- Date for a demo

## Notes from the session
(date, who attended, what you learned, what you changed in the design because of it)
